# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Job Reply Bot is a private tool used by one person, its owner, with the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to the public.

## What Google data it accesses

- Gmail (read-only): reads new messages in the owner's inbox to identify job-booking emails from two specific sender addresses. All other messages are ignored.
- Gmail (send): sends replies to those job-booking emails from the owner's account.
- Google Calendar (read-only): reads the owner's calendar to check availability for the dates and times in a job-booking email.

## How the data is used

The data is used only to decide whether the owner is available for a job, to send the reply, and to notify the owner about job emails (for example, the job dates) through a notification service the owner uses. Nothing is used for advertising, sold, or shared with anyone else.

## Storage

The tool runs in the owner's own Google Cloud project. It holds a short-lived copy of the owner's calendar availability in memory and writes operational logs, such as timings and job dates, to Google Cloud Logging in the owner's project. It does not keep copies of emails.

## Limited Use

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Contact

Questions can be raised through the owner's GitHub profile.
